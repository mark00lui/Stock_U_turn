## Sector overview

Three macro themes dominate the 5/1 setup:

1. **AI Capex Cycle remains in expansion** — TSMC 2nm/CoWoS ramp drives semi-equipment, ABF substrate, advanced packaging, copper foil, thermal management. PCB 載板鏈 today shows **two consecutive days of ETF accumulation** (4/30→5/1) on 欣興/日月光投控/高力 — the strongest institutional confirmation of this theme since the cycle began.
2. **Memory cycle (DRAM/NAND) firmly in mid-expansion** — 旺宏/晶豪科/群聯 spot pricing +25-40% YoY, channel inventory at 6-quarter low.
3. **Industrial automation & robotics theme accelerating** — 大量/台灣精銳 (減速機), 所羅門 (AI 視覺), 由田 (AOI), 4576 大銀微 (線性傳動) — labor-cost pressure + semi factory build-out catalyse Q2 orders.

Defensive sectors (內需零售, 化工, 電信) are flat — not where today's alpha lives.
