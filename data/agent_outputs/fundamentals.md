## Market overview

The TW corporate earnings landscape continues its bifurcation. **AI/HPC supply chain** (PCB/ABF substrate, advanced packaging, semi equipment) prints 30-60% YoY revenue growth; **DRAM/NAND** is in a clean upcycle (旺宏/晶豪科/群聯). **Semi-equipment sub-suppliers** (家登, 辛耘, 志聖, 大銀微, 由田) see back-to-back double-digit YoY months on TSMC capex re-acceleration. Defensive plays (台肥, 中華化, 寶雅) have stable but unspectacular fundamentals. Notable: **欣興 (3037)** and **高力 (8996)** show A-grade fundamentals AND back-to-back 統一 ETF accumulation — strongest cross-confirmation in today's universe.
